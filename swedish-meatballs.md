# 🇸🇪 Swedish Meatballs

Beef and pork meatballs with allspice and nutmeg, simmered in a creamy beef gravy with soy sauce and Dijon.

## 📋 All Ingredients

**Meatballs:**
* 450 g ground beef
* 225 g ground pork
* 30 g breadcrumbs
* 60 ml milk
* 1 small onion, finely chopped
* 1 garlic clove, minced
* 1 egg
* 1 tsp salt
* 0.5 tsp black pepper
* 0.5 tsp allspice
* 0.25 tsp nutmeg
* 2 tbsp butter
* Dash of oil

**Cream Gravy:**
* 3 tbsp butter
* 3 tbsp plain flour
* 320 ml beef stock
* 160 ml double cream
* 2 tsp soy sauce
* 1 tsp Dijon mustard

**To Serve:**
* Fresh parsley, chopped

## Meatballs

### 📋 Ingredients

* 450 g ground beef
* 225 g ground pork
* 30 g breadcrumbs
* 60 ml milk
* 1 small onion, finely chopped
* 1 garlic clove, minced
* 1 egg
* 1 tsp salt
* 0.5 tsp black pepper
* 0.5 tsp allspice
* 0.25 tsp nutmeg
* 2 tbsp butter
* Dash of oil

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Soak the Breadcrumbs:**

   * In a small bowl, combine breadcrumbs and milk. Let sit for 5 minutes.

2. **Mix the Meat:**

   * In a large bowl, combine beef, pork, soaked breadcrumbs, onion, garlic, egg, salt, pepper, allspice, and nutmeg.
   * Mix until just combined — overmixing makes the meatballs dense.

3. **Shape:**

   * Roll into small meatballs, about 2.5 cm in diameter.

4. **Brown:**

   * Melt butter with a dash of oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
   * Brown the meatballs in batches on all sides (about 8–10 minutes per batch). They don't need to be cooked through yet.
   * Remove and set aside. Keep the skillet with its browned bits for the gravy.

---

## Cream Gravy

### 📋 Ingredients

* 3 tbsp butter
* 3 tbsp plain flour
* 320 ml beef stock
* 160 ml double cream
* 2 tsp soy sauce
* 1 tsp Dijon mustard

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Make the Roux:**

   * In the same skillet, melt butter over medium heat.
   * Whisk in flour and stir continuously for about 2 minutes, until lightly golden.

2. **Add the Stock:**

   * Gradually pour in beef stock, whisking constantly to avoid lumps and scraping up the browned bits from the meatballs.

3. **Finish the Gravy:**

   * Stir in cream, soy sauce, and Dijon mustard.
   * Bring to a gentle simmer and stir until the sauce starts to thicken.

4. **Simmer the Meatballs:**

   * Return the meatballs to the skillet.
   * Simmer for 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the meatballs are cooked through and the gravy coats the back of a spoon.
   * Taste and adjust seasoning — the soy sauce adds salt, so season at the end.

---

### 🍽 Serving Suggestion

Serve hot, garnished with fresh parsley. Classic with mashed potatoes and lingonberry jam.
