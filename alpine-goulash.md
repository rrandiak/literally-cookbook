## Goulash

### 📋 Ingredients

* 2 tbsp lard or vegetable oil
* 2 large onions, finely diced
* 1 garlic clove, minced
* 800–1000 g beef chuck or shoulder, cut into 2–3 cm cubes
* 1.5 tbsp sweet paprika (Hungarian or Austrian)
* 1 tsp smoked paprika *(optional)*
* 1 tsp caraway seeds, crushed
* 1 tsp marjoram
* 2 tbsp tomato paste
* 500 ml beef stock
* 250 ml dry red wine
* 1 bay leaf
* Salt and pepper to taste
* Splash of vinegar or lemon juice to finish *(optional)*

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Sauté the Onions:**

   * Heat lard or oil in a large heavy pot over medium heat.
   * Add onions and cook until golden and soft (about 10–15 minutes).

2. **Brown the Meat:**

   * Add garlic and beef. Brown the meat on all sides.

3. **Add Spices:**

   * Stir in tomato paste, sweet and smoked paprika, caraway seeds, and marjoram. Mix well.

4. **Add Liquids:**

   * Pour in beef stock and red wine. Add bay leaf.
   * Season with salt and pepper.

5. **Braise:**

   * Bring to a simmer, then cover and cook on low heat for about 2 hours, until the beef is tender. Stir occasionally.
   * If needed, uncover for the last 20–30 minutes to thicken the sauce.

6. **Finish:**

   * Optional: Add a splash of vinegar or lemon juice at the end for brightness.

## Potato Dumplings (Kartoffelknödel)

### 📋 Ingredients

* 1 kg starchy potatoes (e.g., Russet)
* 1 egg, beaten
* 3–4 tbsp potato starch or flour (more if needed)
* 0.5 tsp salt
* Pinch of nutmeg *(optional)*
* Fresh **chives**, finely chopped — for garnish
* 50–100 g bacon, finely diced

### ⚠️ Note

Depending on the moisture of the potatoes, **you may need significantly more flour** than the base amount listed. Add more gradually until the dough is firm enough to shape but still soft and tender.

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Cook the Potatoes:**

   * Boil the potatoes in their skins until fork-tender.
   * Let cool slightly, peel, and mash or rice them. Let cool completely before mixing.

2. **Fry the Bacon:**

   * Fry the diced bacon in a dry pan over medium heat until just golden and lightly crisp. Set aside to cool.

3. **Make the Dough:**

   * Combine the mashed potatoes with the beaten egg, salt, nutmeg (if using), starch/flour, and cooled bacon.
   * Mix gently into a soft dough — not sticky. Add a little more flour if needed.

4. **Shape & Cook:**

   * With wet hands, form into 6–8 round dumplings.
   * Simmer gently in salted water (do **not** boil hard) for 15–20 minutes. They will float when done.

5. **Serve:**

   * Remove with a slotted spoon and garnish with chopped chives before serving.

---

### 🍽 Serving Suggestion

Plate the dumplings and ladle the goulash over them. Sprinkle fresh chives generously on the dumplings. Best enjoyed with a cold beer or a glass of light red wine.

---

### 💡 Tips

- You can use 1–2 tablespoons of mashed potatoes to help **thicken the goulash** during the final 10 minutes of cooking.
