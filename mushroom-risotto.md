# Mushroom Risotto with Spinach & Chickpeas

A hearty, creamy risotto with earthy mushrooms, wilted spinach, and chickpeas. Swap chickpeas for chicken for a meatier version.

## Risotto

### 📋 Ingredients

* 200 g risotto rice (e.g., Arborio)
* 250 g mushrooms (champignons or mixed), sliced
* 450 g fresh spinach
* 200 ml heavy cream
* 250 g cooked chickpeas (canned, drained and rinsed) — *or 400 g chicken breast, diced*
* 1 shallot, finely diced
* 1 parsley root, peeled and finely diced
* 3 garlic cloves, minced
* 2 tbsp tomato paste
* Juice of 0.5 lemon
* 0.5 tsp ground cumin
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 600–800 ml vegetable broth (or chicken broth if using chicken), kept warm

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Sauté the Base:**

   * Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a wide, heavy pan over medium heat.
   * Add shallot and parsley root and cook for 3–4 minutes until softened.
   * Add garlic and cook for 1 minute. If using chicken, add now and cook until browned on the outside.

2. **Cook the Mushrooms:**

   * Add mushrooms and cook for 4–5 minutes until they release their liquid and start to colour.
   * Stir in tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.

3. **Toast the Rice:**

   * Add the rice and stir to coat in the oil. Toast for 1–2 minutes.

4. **Build the Risotto:**

   * Add warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring frequently and waiting until each addition is absorbed before adding the next.
   * Continue for 18–20 minutes until the rice is creamy and al dente.

5. **Finish:**

   * Stir in chickpeas (or ensure chicken is cooked through). Add spinach in batches, folding it in until wilted.
   * Pour in heavy cream, stir through, and cook for 2 more minutes.
   * Season with cumin, salt, pepper, and a squeeze of lemon juice. Taste and adjust.

### 💡 Tips

- Keep the broth warm in a separate pot — adding cold broth slows cooking and affects texture.
- The risotto should flow slowly when spooned onto a plate (all'onda). If it looks stiff, add a splash more broth.

### 🍽 Serving Suggestion

Serve immediately in warmed bowls. Finish with a squeeze of lemon and a drizzle of olive oil. Pairs well with crusty bread.
