# Placky (Czech Potato Pancakes)

Crispy pan-fried potato pancakes with garlic — a Czech staple. Serve as a side or on their own with sour cream.

## Placky

### 📋 Ingredients

* 8 medium potatoes (about 1–1.2 kg), peeled
* 1 medium onion
* 4 garlic cloves, minced or pressed
* 2 eggs
* 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
* 1 tsp salt
* Freshly ground black pepper
* Vegetable oil, for frying

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Grate & Drain:**

   * Grate the potatoes on the coarse side of a box grater. Grate the onion on the same side.
   * Squeeze the grated potato and onion in a clean kitchen towel to remove as much liquid as possible — this is key to crispiness.

2. **Mix the Batter:**

   * In a large bowl, combine the drained potato-onion mixture with minced garlic, eggs, flour, salt, and a generous amount of black pepper. Mix well.

3. **Fry:**

   * Heat a generous layer of vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
   * Drop heaped spoonfuls of the mixture into the pan and flatten gently into rounds about 1 cm thick.
   * Fry for 3–4 minutes per side until deep golden and crispy. Work in batches — don't crowd the pan.

4. **Drain & Serve:**

   * Drain briefly on paper towels and serve hot.

### 💡 Tips

- Removing liquid from the potatoes is the most important step. Wet potato mixture leads to soggy, oily placky.
- If the mixture feels too wet after squeezing, add 1 more tbsp of flour.
- Best eaten immediately while hot and crispy.

### 🍽 Serving Suggestion

Serve with sour cream, apple sauce, or as a side to roasted meats.
