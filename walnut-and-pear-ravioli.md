# Walnut & Pear Ravioli with Brown Butter, Arugula & Cherry Tomatoes

A sweet-savory ravioli with creamy ricotta, ripe pears, toasted walnuts, and a nutty brown butter sauce finished with peppery arugula and blistered cherry tomatoes.

## 📋 All Ingredients

**Dough:**
* 300 g "00" flour (or all-purpose flour)
* 3 large eggs
* 1 tsp olive oil
* Pinch of salt

**Filling:**
* 2 ripe but firm pears, peeled and finely diced
* 250 g ricotta
* 60 g toasted walnuts, finely chopped
* 50 g grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
* 1 small shallot, finely minced
* 1 tbsp butter
* 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
* Pinch of nutmeg
* Salt and black pepper
* Drizzle of honey *(optional)*

**Sauce:**
* 120 g butter
* 2 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
* 200 g cherry tomatoes, halved
* 2 handfuls arugula
* 1 tbsp chopped toasted walnuts *(optional)*
* Juice of 0.5 lemon
* Fresh black pepper
* Parmesan for serving

---

## Ravioli Dough

### 📋 Ingredients

* 300 g "00" flour (or all-purpose flour)
* 3 large eggs
* 1 tsp olive oil
* Pinch of salt

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Make the Well:**

   * Place flour on a work surface and make a well in the center.
   * Add eggs, olive oil, and salt into the well.

2. **Form the Dough:**

   * Gradually incorporate the flour from the edges until a dough forms.

3. **Knead & Rest:**

   * Knead for 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
   * Wrap and let rest for 30–45 minutes.

---

## Filling

### 📋 Ingredients

* 2 ripe but firm pears, peeled and finely diced
* 250 g ricotta
* 60 g toasted walnuts, finely chopped
* 50 g grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
* 1 small shallot, finely minced
* 1 tbsp butter
* 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
* Pinch of nutmeg
* Salt and black pepper
* Drizzle of honey *(optional)*

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Toast the Walnuts:**

   * Toast walnuts in a dry skillet until fragrant. Cool and chop.

2. **Cook the Pear:**

   * Melt butter in a pan and gently cook shallot until soft.
   * Add pears and thyme. Cook 3–4 minutes until softened but not mushy.
   * Let the pear mixture cool completely.

3. **Combine:**

   * Mix cooled pear mixture with ricotta, walnuts, Parmesan, nutmeg, salt, and pepper.
   * Taste and adjust seasoning. The filling should be creamy but not watery.

---

## Assemble the Ravioli

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Roll the Dough:**

   * Roll pasta sheets thin using a pasta machine or rolling pin.

2. **Fill & Seal:**

   * Place small spoonfuls of filling evenly across one sheet.
   * Brush around filling with water.
   * Cover with another pasta sheet and press out air pockets.
   * Cut ravioli and seal edges firmly.

3. **Rest:**

   * Place on a semolina-dusted tray.

---

## Brown Butter Sauce with Arugula & Cherry Tomatoes

### 📋 Ingredients

* 120 g butter
* 2 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
* 200 g cherry tomatoes, halved
* 2 handfuls arugula
* 1 tbsp chopped toasted walnuts *(optional)*
* Juice of 0.5 lemon
* Fresh black pepper
* Parmesan for serving

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Boil Water:**

   * Bring a large pot of salted water to a gentle boil.

2. **Brown the Butter:**

   * Melt butter in a large pan over medium heat.
   * Cook until butter turns golden brown and smells nutty.
   * Add garlic and cherry tomatoes. Cook until tomatoes soften and lightly blister.
   * Add lemon juice and black pepper.

3. **Cook & Toss the Ravioli:**

   * Cook ravioli for 2–3 minutes until they float.
   * Transfer ravioli directly into the sauce. Toss gently to coat.

4. **Finish:**

   * Fold in arugula just before serving so it lightly wilts.
   * Serve immediately with extra Parmesan and toasted walnuts.

---

### 💡 Optional Variations

- Add a little Gorgonzola to the filling for a stronger savory contrast.
- Crispy sage leaves pair beautifully with the brown butter.
- Substitute spinach for arugula if preferred.
- Finish with thin fresh pear slices for extra elegance.
