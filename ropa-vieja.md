## Ropa Vieja

### 📋 Ingredients

* 900–1350 g beef (flank steak, chuck roast, or brisket)
* 750–1000 ml water or beef broth
* 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
* 3 tbsp olive oil
* 1 medium yellow onion, diced
* 1 large green bell pepper, sliced
* 1 large red bell pepper, sliced
* 4–6 garlic cloves, minced
* 400 g crushed tomatoes
* 2 tbsp tomato paste
* 240 ml beef broth
* 1–2 tbsp blended fresh or dried chilies, adjust for heat
* 1.5 tsp ground cumin
* 1 tsp dried oregano
* 2 bay leaves
* 80–100 g pimiento-stuffed olives, sliced *(optional)*
* 2 tbsp capers, drained *(optional)*
* Freshly ground black pepper to taste

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Prep the Chilies:**

   * If using fresh or dried chilies, blend 240 ml beef broth with 1–2 tbsp chilies to a smooth sauce; set aside.

2. **Brown the Aromatics:**

   * Heat 3 tbsp olive oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat.
   * Add diced onion and cook 4–5 minutes until translucent.
   * Add minced garlic and cook 30–60 seconds.
   * Add sliced bell peppers and cook 3–4 minutes until slightly softened.
   * Stir in tomato paste and cook 1 minute to caramelise.

3. **Build the Sauce:**

   * Pour in the blended chili-broth (or plain broth if not using chilies).
   * Add crushed tomatoes, cumin, oregano, bay leaves, olives and capers if using, and 1 tsp salt. Stir to combine.

4. **Braise:**

   * Nestle the whole beef into the sauce so liquid comes about halfway up the meat. Add extra water or broth as needed (total liquid 750–1000 ml).
   * Bring to a gentle simmer, cover, and reduce heat to low.
   * Braise gently for 2–2.5 hours, turning the meat once at the halfway mark, until very tender and a fork slides in with no resistance.

5. **Shred & Finish:**

   * Remove meat to a cutting board and shred with two forks. Discard any large pieces of fat.
   * Stir shredded beef back into the pot. Simmer uncovered on low for 10–20 minutes so the meat absorbs the sauce. If sauce is too thin, stir in 1 tbsp tomato paste. Remove bay leaves.
   * Taste and adjust salt and black pepper. Finish with a squeeze of lime for brightness if desired.

## White Rice

### 📋 Ingredients

* 300 g long-grain white rice (e.g., jasmine)
* 600 ml water
* 1 tsp salt
* 1 tbsp vegetable oil or butter

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Rinse:**

   * Rinse rice until water runs clear.

2. **Cook:**

   * Bring 600 ml water, 1 tsp salt, and 1 tbsp oil to a boil in a pot.
   * Add rice, stir once, cover, and reduce to low.
   * Simmer 12–15 minutes until water is absorbed.

3. **Rest & Fluff:**

   * Remove from heat and let sit covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.

## Mexican-Style Beans

### 📋 Ingredients

* 400 g cooked black or pinto beans (canned, drained and rinsed)
* 1 tbsp vegetable oil
* 1 small onion, finely chopped
* 2 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 small tomato, diced
* 1 small jalapeño or serrano, seeded and finely chopped *(optional)*
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* 0.5 tsp dried oregano
* 50–100 ml water or bean cooking liquid
* Salt and pepper to taste
* Fresh cilantro *(optional)*

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Sauté the Aromatics:**

   * Heat 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium.
   * Sauté onion for 4–5 minutes until soft.
   * Add garlic, tomato, and chili; cook 2–3 minutes.

2. **Simmer the Beans:**

   * Add beans, cumin, oregano, and 50–100 ml liquid.
   * Simmer 5–10 minutes, mashing lightly with a potato masher for creaminess.
   * Adjust salt and pepper. Stir in cilantro if using.

## Corn & Pepper Mix

### 📋 Ingredients

* 400 g frozen or canned sweet corn, drained
* 1 tbsp vegetable oil
* 1 small onion, finely diced
* 1 red bell pepper, diced
* 2 tomatoes, diced
* 0.5 tsp smoked paprika *(optional)*
* Salt and pepper to taste
* 1 tbsp chopped cilantro or parsley *(optional)*
* 1 lime, cut into wedges

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Sauté the Aromatics:**

   * Heat 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium-high.
   * Sauté onion 3–4 minutes until translucent.
   * Add red pepper and cook 3 minutes.

2. **Add Corn & Tomatoes:**

   * Add corn and tomatoes, cook 4–6 minutes until warmed and slightly reduced.
   * Season with smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
   * Stir in cilantro and serve with lime wedges.

---

### 🍽 Serving Suggestion

Spoon rice onto a plate, ladle ropa vieja over or to the side, and serve beans and corn alongside. Garnish with cilantro and lime wedges. Fried plantains are an excellent addition if available.
