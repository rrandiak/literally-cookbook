# Flour Tortillas

Soft homemade flour tortillas. Two variations: plain flour or a flour-corn blend for a slightly more rustic flavour.

## 📋 All Ingredients

**Plain Flour Version:**
* 360 g all-purpose flour
* 1 tbsp baking powder
* 1 tsp salt
* 80 g butter, melted
* 240 ml hot water

**Flour & Corn Version:**
* 270 g all-purpose flour
* 90 g fine cornmeal (masa harina or fine polenta)
* 1 tbsp baking powder
* 1 tsp salt
* 80 g butter, melted
* 270 ml hot water

---

## Plain Flour Tortillas

### 📋 Ingredients

* 360 g all-purpose flour
* 1 tbsp baking powder
* 1 tsp salt
* 80 g butter, melted
* 240 ml hot water

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Make the Dough:**

   * In a large bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt.
   * Add melted butter and mix until crumbly.
   * Pour in hot water gradually, stirring until a dough forms.

2. **Knead & Rest:**

   * Knead for 2–3 minutes until smooth. Divide into 8 equal balls.
   * Cover with a clean towel and rest for 10 minutes.

3. **Roll & Cook:**

   * On a lightly floured surface, roll each ball out as thin as possible into a rough circle (about 20–25 cm).
   * Heat a dry skillet or cast-iron pan over medium-high heat. Cook each tortilla for 30–60 seconds per side until light golden spots appear.

4. **Keep Warm:**

   * Stack and cover with a towel to keep warm and pliable.

---

## Flour & Corn Tortillas

### 📋 Ingredients

* 270 g all-purpose flour
* 90 g fine cornmeal (masa harina or fine polenta)
* 1 tbsp baking powder
* 1 tsp salt
* 80 g butter, melted
* 270 ml hot water

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Make the Dough:**

   * In a large bowl, whisk together flour, cornmeal, baking powder, and salt.
   * Add melted butter and mix until crumbly.
   * Pour in hot water gradually, stirring until a dough forms. The dough will be slightly coarser than the plain version.

2. **Knead & Rest:**

   * Knead for 2–3 minutes until smooth. Divide into 8 equal balls.
   * Cover with a clean towel and rest for 10 minutes.

3. **Roll & Cook:**

   * On a lightly floured surface, roll each ball out as thin as possible into a rough circle (about 20–25 cm).
   * Heat a dry skillet or cast-iron pan over medium-high heat. Cook each tortilla for 30–60 seconds per side until light golden spots appear.

4. **Keep Warm:**

   * Stack and cover with a towel to keep warm and pliable.

---

### 💡 Tips

- Rest the dough — it makes rolling much easier.
- Don't overcook; a few golden spots are ideal. Overcooked tortillas turn crisp and crack when folded.
- Tortillas keep well wrapped in a towel for a few hours, or stacked and refrigerated for up to 3 days.
