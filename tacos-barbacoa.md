# Tacos Barbacoa

Slow-braised spiced beef, shredded and served in warm tortillas.

## 📋 All Ingredients

* 800 g beef chuck or brisket (or 500 g beef + 1 can Bonduelle Mexicana corn-pepper mix)
* 500 ml beef broth
* 2 dried or fresh chilies (e.g., ancho or guajillo), stems and seeds removed
* 1 tbsp ground cumin
* 1 tsp dried oregano
* 4 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 medium onion, roughly chopped
* 2 tbsp tomato paste
* 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar or lime juice
* 1 tsp salt
* Freshly ground black pepper

**To serve:**
* Tortillas (see tortillas.md)
* Fresh cilantro, chopped
* White onion, finely diced
* Lime wedges
* Salsa verde (see salsa-verde.md)

## Barbacoa Beef

### 📋 Ingredients

* 800 g beef chuck or brisket
* 500 ml beef broth
* 2 dried or fresh chilies, stems and seeds removed
* 1 tbsp ground cumin
* 1 tsp dried oregano
* 4 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 medium onion, roughly chopped
* 2 tbsp tomato paste
* 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar or lime juice
* 1 tsp salt
* Freshly ground black pepper

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Prep the Chilies:**

   * If using dried chilies, soak in 100 ml of hot water for 10 minutes, then blend with the soaking liquid to a paste.

2. **Braise:**

   * In a large heavy pot, combine beef, beef broth, chili paste (or fresh chilies blended with a splash of broth), cumin, oregano, garlic, onion, tomato paste, vinegar, salt, and pepper.
   * Bring to a simmer, cover, and braise on low heat for 2–2.5 hours until the beef is very tender and falls apart.

3. **Shred & Finish:**

   * Remove beef from the pot and shred with two forks. Return shredded beef to the pot and stir to coat with the braising liquid.
   * Simmer uncovered for 10 minutes to reduce and intensify the sauce. Adjust salt to taste.

### 💡 Tips

- For a lighter version, replace half the beef with a can of Bonduelle Mexicana (corn, pepper, and bean mix) — add it in the last 10 minutes of simmering after shredding the meat.

---

### 🍽 Serving Suggestion

Warm the tortillas. Fill each with a generous spoonful of barbacoa. Top with fresh cilantro, diced white onion, and a squeeze of lime. Serve with salsa verde on the side.
