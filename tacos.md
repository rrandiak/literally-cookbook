# Oven-Baked Tacos

Filled tacos arranged in a baking dish and baked until the cheese is melted and the shells are slightly crisp. Use store-bought or homemade tortillas.

## 📋 All Ingredients

* 4–6 small flour or corn tortillas (store-bought or see tortillas.md)
* 80–100 g cheddar or mozzarella, grated
* A handful of micro greens
* Pickled red onions, to taste
* Salsa verde or your preferred sauce (see salsa-verde.md)

**Simple beef filling:**
* 300 g ground beef
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* 0.5 tsp smoked paprika
* 0.5 tsp chili powder
* Salt and pepper
* 1 tbsp tomato paste

---

## Taco Filling

### 📋 Ingredients

* 300 g ground beef (or leftover barbacoa — see tacos-barbacoa.md)
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* 0.5 tsp smoked paprika
* 0.5 tsp chili powder
* Salt and pepper
* 1 tbsp tomato paste

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Brown the Beef:**

   * Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it cooks.

2. **Season & Finish:**

   * Season with cumin, smoked paprika, chili powder, salt, and pepper.
   * Stir in tomato paste and cook for 1–2 minutes. Remove from heat.

---

## Assembly & Baking

### 📋 Ingredients

* 4–6 small flour or corn tortillas
* Prepared filling (above)
* 80–100 g cheddar or mozzarella, grated
* A handful of micro greens
* Pickled red onions, to taste

### 🍴 Instructions

1. **Preheat & Prep:**

   * Preheat oven to 200 °C.
   * Warm tortillas briefly in a dry pan or microwave so they are pliable.

2. **Fill:**

   * Spoon filling down the centre of each tortilla. Top with grated cheese.
   * Fold tacos in half and arrange snugly in an ovenproof glass or ceramic dish so they hold each other upright.

3. **Bake:**

   * Bake for 5–10 minutes until cheese is melted and the tortilla edges are lightly crisp.

4. **Top & Serve:**

   * Remove from oven. Top with micro greens and pickled red onions.

### 🍽 Serving Suggestion

Serve straight from the dish with salsa verde or sour cream on the side.
